# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want eWash to be a respectful, practical, and productive project for contributors. We commit to making participation welcoming and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

Contributors are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Give clear technical feedback without personal attacks.
- Assume good intent while still being honest about risks.
- Keep discussions focused on the work.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.
- Handle security reports responsibly.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Public sharing of private information.
- Threats or intimidation.
- Deliberate disruption of project discussions.
- Publishing secrets, customer data, or vulnerability details irresponsibly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its contributors.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Include relevant context and links where possible.
